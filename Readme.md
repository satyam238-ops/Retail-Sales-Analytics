# 🛒 Retail Sales Analytics & Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail product and store-level sales data to uncover the key factors influencing **product-store sales performance**.

The analysis focuses on relationships between product characteristics, pricing, store characteristics, location, shelf allocation, and overall sales.

The goal is to transform raw retail data into **actionable business insights** that can help retailers understand product performance, store performance, pricing behavior, and sales drivers.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer the following questions:

* Which products generate the highest total sales?
* Does higher MRP lead to higher or lower sales?
* Does product weight influence sales?
* Does sugar content have an impact on sales?
* Does store age affect sales performance?
* Which store sizes generate the highest sales?
* Which store type performs best?
* Which products are the top contributors to store sales?
* Which numerical variables have the strongest relationship with sales?

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains product-level and store-level information.

### Dataset Features

| Feature                     | Description                                       |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `product_id`                | Unique identifier for each product                |
| `product_sugar_content`     | Sugar-content category of the product             |
| `product_type`              | Category/type of the product                      |
| `store_id`                  | Unique identifier for each store                  |
| `store_size`                | Size category of the store                        |
| `store_location_city_type`  | Type/category of the store's city location        |
| `store_type`                | Type of retail store                              |
| `product_weight`            | Weight of the product                             |
| `product_allocated_area`    | Area allocated to the product in the store        |
| `product_mrp`               | Maximum Retail Price of the product               |
| `store_establishment_year`  | Year in which the store was established           |
| `product_store_sales_total` | Total sales generated by the product in the store |

# 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis

The project uses exploratory data analysis to understand the relationships between product attributes, store characteristics, and sales.

---

## 1. 🔥 Correlation Analysis

A correlation analysis was performed on the numerical variables to identify relationships between product/store attributes and total sales.

The analysis helps identify whether variables such as:

* Product MRP
* Product Weight
* Product Allocated Area
* Store Establishment Year
* Total Sales

have positive or negative relationships.

### Key Question

> Which numerical factors have the strongest relationship with product-store sales?

---

## 2. 🏢 Store Age vs Sales

Store age was derived from the store establishment year and analyzed against total sales.

### Key Question

> Do older stores generate more sales, or do newer stores perform better?

This analysis helps understand whether store maturity is associated with sales performance.

---

## 3. 💰 MRP vs Sales

The relationship between **Product MRP** and **Total Sales** was analyzed using a scatter/line visualization.

### Key Question

> Does a higher product price result in higher or lower sales?

This can help identify pricing patterns and understand how product price relates to consumer purchasing behavior.

---

## 4. 🍬 Sugar Content vs Sales

Sales performance was compared across different product sugar-content categories.

### Key Question

> Does product sugar content have a meaningful relationship with sales?

This analysis can help identify whether certain sugar-content categories are more commercially successful.

---

## 5. ⚖️ Product Weight vs Sales

Product weight was compared with total sales to investigate whether heavier or lighter products tend to generate higher sales.

### Key Question

> Is product weight associated with sales performance?

---

## 6. 🏆 Top 8 Products by Total Sales

The top-performing products were identified based on their total sales contribution.

### Key Question

> Which products are the strongest contributors to overall store sales?

Identifying the highest-performing products can help retailers prioritize:

* Inventory
* Shelf placement
* Promotions
* Stock availability

---

## 7. 🏪 Store Performance by Store Size

Sales were compared across different store-size categories.

### Key Question

> Which store size generates the highest sales?

This provides insight into whether larger stores necessarily generate higher sales or whether smaller stores can achieve strong sales efficiency.

---

## 8. 🛍️ Sales by Store Type

Store types were compared based on their total sales performance.

### Key Question

> Which type of store generates the highest sales?

This analysis helps identify the strongest retail formats within the dataset.

---
